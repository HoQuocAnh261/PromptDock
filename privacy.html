Privacy Policy

PromptDock Desktop Application • Effective Date: September 14, 2026
PromptDock operates 100% Offline. No data ever leaves your device. Zero cloud sync, zero tracking.

1. Overview

PromptDock ("the Application") is developed with a strict privacy-by-design architecture. We believe your intellectual property, custom AI prompts, variables, and creative workflows belong exclusively to you.

This Privacy Policy explains how PromptDock handles information when you install and use the application via Microsoft Store or as a Progressive Web Application (PWA).

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

Because PromptDock runs entirely client-side without any remote backend servers or third-party SDKs, we do not collect:

  • Personal Information: No names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials.
  • Prompt & Creative Data: All prompts, tags, categories, variable substitutions, and clipboard operations remain strictly in your local browser storage.
  • Telemetry & Analytics: No tracking scripts, Google Analytics, telemetry beacons, or usage monitoring.
  • Device Information: No device fingerprints, IP addresses, MAC addresses, or location data.
  • Cookies & Identifiers: The application does not set or read any advertising or tracking cookies.

3. How Your Data is Stored Locally

All prompts, categories, favorites, and preference settings (theme, font size, language) are stored locally on your device using the browser's standard localStorage API.

This data is sandboxed to your device and is never uploaded to any remote server or third party. You can completely export, backup, or delete your data at any time via the Settings dialog inside the app.

4. Network Permissions & Offline Mode

PromptDock is powered by an offline Service Worker that caches application code (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) on your local hard drive. Once installed, PromptDock can operate with zero internet connectivity.

The application makes zero outbound network calls during regular operation.

5. Data Export, Backup & Deletion Rights

You have total ownership and control over your data:

  • Export: Click Settings ➔ Export JSON to download your complete library as a .json file.
  • Import: Restore your data on any machine via Settings ➔ Import JSON.
  • Deletion: You can wipe all stored data by clicking Settings ➔ Restore Sample Data or by clearing your browser/application storage.

6. Children's Privacy

PromptDock does not collect any data from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The application is completely safe for all audiences.

7. Compliance with Microsoft Store Policy

This policy complies with the Microsoft Store Developer Agreement and App Certification Requirements (Section 10.5.1: Personal Information).

8. Contact & Questions

If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact the development team through the official GitHub repository or your Microsoft Store developer support link.
